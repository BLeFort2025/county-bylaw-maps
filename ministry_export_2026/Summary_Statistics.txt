METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------
The OFA Municipal Bylaw Database tracks all 444 Ontario
municipalities across 7 bylaw categories. DC farm building
exemption status is determined by direct review of enacted
bylaw text (the operative exemption sections), not from
summaries or secondary sources.

Worker housing classification is based on text analysis of
the recorded statutory wording in the database. Municipalities
are classified as:
  - 'Exempt': Operative bylaw text explicitly includes worker
    housing (bunkhouses, farm help quarters, seasonal labourer
    accommodation) in the DC exemption.
  - 'Explicitly Excluded': Operative bylaw text specifically
    carves out worker housing from the farm building exemption.
  - 'Silent (Presumed Not Exempt)': Standard 'non-residential
    farm building' language that does not address worker housing.
    Under the Ontario Building Code definition, worker housing
    is classified as residential occupancy and would not qualify
    as a 'farm building' under this standard language.
